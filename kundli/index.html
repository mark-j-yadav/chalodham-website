How this kundli system works

1. Birth data

Name, date, IST time and any Indian city or district. Time is read as Asia/Kolkata so lagna uses that place’s coordinates.

2. Sidereal sky

Planets are computed astronomically, then shifted by Lahiri ayanamsa into the Vedic (nirayana) zodiac used across North Indian kundli practice.

Kundli analysis FAQs

Is this online janam kundli free?
The birth chart, lagna, rashi, nakshatra, graha table and dasha timeline are free. Advance vishleshan — doshas, upay, naukri/business map and PDF — is ₹29.
What do I get after paying ₹29?
Mangal, Kaal Sarp, Pitra, Kemadruma, Guru-Chandal, Grahan, Angarak and current Sade Sati indications with remedies; what is already shubh; job vs business from the 10th/7th; next mahadasha; and a downloadable PDF. Unlock lasts 90 days on that browser.
How accurate is this Vedic kundli calculator?
The chart uses Lahiri (Chitrapaksha) ayanamsa, whole-sign houses and astronomical planetary longitudes. It is suitable for personal study. A learned jyotishi may refine divisional charts (D9, D10) further.
What birth details are required for kundli vishleshan?
Name, gender, date of birth, time of birth and city. Accurate birth time matters most for lagna (ascendant). If time is unknown, the Moon rashi and nakshatra are still useful.
How do I pay ₹29?
Razorpay (UPI/card) if configured, or any UPI app to ChaloDham. After UPI, paste the UTR to unlock. A WhatsApp note is sent so we can match the payment.
Can I get a Braj yatra planned from my kundli?
Yes. After you generate the kundli, send it on WhatsApp. ChaloDham can suggest Mathura–Vrindavan darshan pacing, rest and muhurat-friendly travel days around your family.
