About Mathura

A living pilgrimage, planned with care

Mathura sits on the Yamuna and is visited by families, first-time pilgrims and returning devotees throughout the year. The city is compact, busy and deeply local — which is why having a calm taxi, a well-placed stay and a realistic itinerary matters.

ChaloDham currently focuses on Mathura and Vrindavan. We help you move between the main temples, the ghats and nearby Braj towns without turning the day into a rush.

Popular places

Shri Krishna Janmabhoomi

The temple complex associated with Krishna’s birth — the spiritual centre of Mathura.

Dwarkadhish Temple

A richly ornamented temple in the old city, often combined with a short walking circuit.

Vishram Ghat

The principal ghat on the Yamuna, especially atmospheric at evening aarti.

Birla Mandir

A quieter, spacious temple often preferred by families looking for an unhurried visit.

Recommended itinerary

  1. 01 · Morning

    Janmabhoomi darshan with an early start to keep queues and heat manageable.

  2. 02 · Midday

    Dwarkadhish and old-city temples, with a pause for a simple local meal.

  3. 03 · Evening

    Vishram Ghat for the riverfront and aarti, then a calm transfer to your stay or Vrindavan.
